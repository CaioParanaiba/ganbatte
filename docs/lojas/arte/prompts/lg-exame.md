ASSET SHEET:
- Item: Legendary Hunter Exam stamp (file: lg-exame.png)
- Type: stamp
- Canvas: square, centered, 12% empty margin.
- Color: one flat ink color per concept (see Variations); no gloss, no highlights, no gradients, no metallic or wax look.
- Freedom: high
- Description: the end-of-season stamp pressed on the hunter license by the whole group after they beat the final Hunter Exam. It must feel heavier and more epic than the common exam stamps, but it is still a STAMP or BRAND printed flat on a paper license card, with the same flat, worn, uneven-ink rendering as the stamp references.
- References: (1) the license card the stamp will be pressed on; (2) two approved stamps. Match ONLY their flat worn-ink rendering and line weight. Do not copy their motifs or colors: motif and color come from the Variations list.
- Avoid: wax seals, 3D, glossy highlights, glow, words, letters, real kanji.
Variations: generate exactly 4 images, one per concept, in this order. They must look very different from each other (different shape, motif and mood):
1. BURNED BRAND: a hot branding-iron mark scorched into the card. Charred near-black brown (#2B1810) with cracked, flaking burnt edges and a few burnt flecks around it (flat tones, no glow). Motif: a tall stepped tower (the Trick Tower) inside a heavy ring.
2. HEAVY ROUND INK STAMP: a large round rubber stamp in deep crimson (#A61E2D), double ring with tick marks all around the ring like a clock or compass, a bold hourglass in the center. Very uneven ink, big gaps where the ink failed.
3. SQUARE SEAL (hanko style): a square seal block pressed in vermilion (#C8102E), carved-seal look (solid red with carved-out lines), a geometric carved knot pattern around a central diamond. Abstract only: no real characters or kanji.
4. STENCIL SPRAY: a bold stencil mark in near-black (#17171B), stencil bridges (gaps) and rough spray speckle around the edges. Motif: a crossed pair of keys over a shield, like a crate marking.
