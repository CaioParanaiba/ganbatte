ASSET SHEET:
- Item: Chain border for UI cards (file: bd-corrente.png)
- Type: border-stretch (a square picture frame made of chain)
- Canvas: square. The chain frame runs along all four edges, close to the edges, about 8% of the width thick, the same on all sides. The whole inside of the frame is flat #FF00FF magenta.
- Sides: each side is a straight chain of the same links, alternating flat and side-on, evenly spaced, so a side can be repeated to make it longer.
- Corners: where the chain turns 90 degrees: one bent link, a bigger link, a padlock or a ring joining the two sides. All four corners identical (rotated).
- Color: fixed palette: white and light-grey steel with a thick dark-grey outline (same as the references)
- Freedom: low on the sides, high on the corners
- Description: Kurapika's chain as a frame around a card, same links as the attached references.
- Avoid: chains crossing over each other at the corners, sagging or curved sides, different link sizes between sides, anything drawn inside the frame.
